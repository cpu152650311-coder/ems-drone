Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be an engineer to work with you?

Most of our customers have an engineering or technical background — but not all. If you can describe your mission requirements (payload mass, endurance, operating conditions), our team can translate that into a component configuration. For complete aircraft, we take care of the engineering detail.

What is the minimum order?

There is no hard minimum. Individual components can be ordered in single units. For custom engineering or complete aircraft, the engagement typically starts from a single prototype unit. Volume discounts apply for production quantities.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship to North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East. Shipping method and cost depend on component type, quantity, and destination. Battery shipments follow IATA Dangerous Goods regulations.

What if a component fails during testing?

All components carry manufacturer warranty. We handle warranty claims on your behalf. For pre-shipment failures caught during our testing, we replace the component before it reaches you — at no additional cost.
